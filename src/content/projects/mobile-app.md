## What it does

Members of a debt-management program can see where their plan stands, make payments and update their
account from their phone, instead of calling a counselor.

## What I built

- Screens and flows in FlutterFlow, with custom Flutter code where the low-code tool ran out.
- Secure sign-in with refresh-token auth, and co-applicant support so couples can share one program.
- In-app payments through the company's payment processor, the same integration as the [payment portal](/projects/payments/).
- QA and the release process for both stores.

I later built three more apps on the same foundation: Consolidated Credit Canada, New Leaf Debt Solutions
and the Lighthouse Finance member portal.
