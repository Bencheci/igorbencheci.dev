## What it is

The long-form version of the "talk to a counselor" flow. Instead of a contact form, visitors answer a guided
set of questions; at the end a soft credit pull (no impact on their score) returns their actual debts, and the
wizard shows a debt-relief quote they can act on right away.

## What I built

A React 19 + Vite app with 17 steps, validation and resume, embedded on consolidatedcredit.org, wired to the
credit-report API and the lead pipeline.
