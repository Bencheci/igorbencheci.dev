Sprint was the exclusive wireless partner of Pokémon GO and wanted to bring players into its 10,500+ stores.
The agency built a rewards program open to anyone in the US: players earned points for in-game milestones,
catching Pokémon at Sprint stores and learning about Sprint, picked a team, climbed a real-time leaderboard
and redeemed prizes. Store staff across the country could publish local events that players RSVP'd to.

I worked on parts of the platform and kept it running after launch. Later we reused the same platform for
a 2018 World Cup rewards program, which I built myself. Fans predicted match scores and won points for
correct guesses, then redeemed them for Amazon, Google Play or Apple gift cards. I integrated a live scores
API so results and points updated in real time during each match.

Results from the award entry: over a billion points in nine months, 766K+ prizes, 22M+ activities, more
than 60% of members who weren't Sprint customers, and no paid traffic.
