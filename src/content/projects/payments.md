## What it is

Members pay their monthly program deposit online. I integrated the payment processor's APIs into every place a
member, or an agent on the phone with them, might take a payment: the member portal (two phases), the
[mobile app](/projects/mobile-app/), the redesigned Solutions portal and a portal agents use for New Leaf and Consolidated Credit Canada.

## What I did

- Payment screens and the API layer behind them.
- Localization for Canadian members in English, French and Spanish.
- Documentation and production deploys.

Details of the processor integration are kept general here on purpose.
